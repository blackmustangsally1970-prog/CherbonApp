Cherbon Waters Garden Weddings & Marquee Receptions
Appointments, Viewings & Rehearsals
Cherbon Waters is viewed by appointment only. Initial viewings are complimentary. Additional meetings or personal appointments incur a $95 fee. Rehearsals are held on Thursdays during business hours, (limited to 45 minutes). Celebrants, photographers and family members wishing to inspect the venue should attend the scheduled rehearsal appointment.
Booking Confirmations
Reception bookings require a $2,200 non-refundable booking fee and signed Terms & Conditions within seven days. Ceremony-only bookings must be paid in full at the time of booking. A booking is not secured until all required payments and documentation are received. Payment via Bank Transfer or Cash only. EFTPOS is not available.
Cancellation/Change Policy
Cancellation of a booking does not entitle the client to a refund of monies paid. This includes booking deposits, progress payments, vendor payments, and any other amounts paid to Cherbon Waters.
Transfer of Booking Date
Wedding dates may be postponed once, no later than 6 months prior to your wedding for any reason, and move to an available date within 12 months of the original booking. All bookings must be notified in writing and fee of $500 will be applicable. If less than 6 months notice is given then the booking fee will be forfeited.
Time Slots
To ensure fairness to all clients and events scheduled on the same day, all agreed arrival and departure times must be strictly observed. Cherbon Waters reserves the right to charge additional fees where the venue is used beyond the contracted event times.Pre-arranged extensions beyond the standard 10:00pm finish time are available at a cost of $1,895 per additional hour, with the latest finish time being 11:00pm. To facilitate a timely departure, last drinks will be called 30 minutes prior to the scheduled finish time, the bar will close 20 minutes prior, and the bridal party is expected to depart 15 minutes prior to the contracted finish time, allowing guests to exit the venue by the agreed departure time. Where clients, guests or vendors fail to comply with these departure procedures and the venue remains occupied beyond the contracted finish time, an additional fee of $880 will apply.
Catering
Outside catering is not permitted. For Receptions – Any outside food (such as your wedding cake, edible components of bonbonniere etc.) must be provided by a licensed food vendor.
Children
Children under 12 receive a $30 discount from full pricing (includes table setting). Babies and toddlers not requiring a meal or seat are not charged. High chairs are not supplied.
Wedding Cake
Our chef would like to make you aware that when you supply your own wedding cake it can only be delivered to the marquee an hour before your reception at the earliest. There will be no price reduction for supplying your own cake as dessert.
Confetti
Natural Rose petals and bubbles are accepted. Confetti, rice and preserved/biodegradable fake rose petals/products or any other items are not accepted. (Exception: Rice allowed for traditional Indian Cultural weddings
Alcohol & Drinks
All service of alcohol and drinks is bar service only. Table service (including table glassware) can be arranged at an additional charge with Cherbon Waters management. Cherbon Waters is a BYO facility. Spirits are only permitted to be consumed in the marquee and will be served from behind the bar. Supply of any spirits will incur an additional corkage fee of $440 (premixed spirits do not attract this charge) All drinks are to be delivered COLD to the venue if delivering on the morning of the wedding . Any opened drinks must be consumed or discarded prior to leaving the premises in accordance with Liquor Licensing laws. Responsible Service of Alcohol standards are applicable at all times.
Music
Music must not exceed 80 decibels, and bass levels must be kept to a minimum at all times. You must inform your DJ, band, musician, or other entertainers of this requirement prior to the event. Cherbon Waters reserves the right to request that volume levels be reduced if necessary. Should this requirement not be complied with, our Wedding Manager reserves the right to cease the music immediately. This condition is enforced in accordance with local noise restriction regulations.
Smoking & Vaping
Smoking and Vaping is permitted only within designated areas and prohibited in all buildings, gazebos and marquees.

All supplier equipment, decorations and materials must be removed from the venue on the night of the event immediately following its conclusion. Suppliers are allocated up to 30 minutes following the conclusion of the event to complete pack-down, remove equipment and vacate the venue. Should a supplier require additional pack-down time, prior arrangements must be made with management. Any applicable extended access fees must be approved and paid in full prior to the event.
