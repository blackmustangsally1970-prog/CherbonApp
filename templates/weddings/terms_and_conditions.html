Terms & Conditions

Cherbon Waters Garden Weddings & Marquee Receptions
Appointments, Viewings & Rehearsals
Cherbon Waters is viewed by appointment only. Initial viewings are complimentary. Additional meetings or personal appointments incur a $95 fee. Rehearsals are held on Thursdays during business hours, limited to 45 minutes. Celebrants, photographers and family members wishing to inspect the venue should attend the scheduled rehearsal appointment.
Booking Confirmations
Reception bookings require a $2,200 non-refundable booking fee and signed Terms & Conditions within seven days. Ceremony-only bookings must be paid in full at the time of booking. A booking is not secured until all required payments and documentation are received. Payment via Bank Transfer or Cash only. EFTPOS is not available.
Cancellation Policy
No refunds for any money paid to Cherbon Waters are given for cancellation of bookings.
Transfer of Booking Date
Wedding dates may be postponed once, no later than 6 months prior to your wedding for any reason, and moved to an available date within 12 months of the original booking. All bookings must be notified in writing and a fee of $500 will apply. If less than 6 months notice is given then the booking fee will be forfeited.
Time Slots
In an effort to be fair to all parties booked on the same day, it is requested that arrival and departure times are strictly observed. Cherbon Waters reserves the right to invoice clients for extended usage. To extend your time past 10pm the cost is $1,895 for an additional hour until 11pm.
Catering
Outside catering is not permitted. For receptions, any outside food such as wedding cake or edible bonbonniere components must be provided by a licensed food vendor.
Children
Children under 12 receive a $30 discount from full pricing including table setting. Babies and toddlers not requiring a meal or seat are not charged. High chairs are not supplied.
Wedding Cake
When supplying your own wedding cake it may only be delivered to the marquee one hour before your reception at the earliest. There is no price reduction for supplying your own cake as dessert.
Confetti
Natural rose petals and bubbles are permitted. Confetti, rice, preserved or biodegradable artificial petals and similar products are not permitted. Exception: Rice is permitted for traditional Indian cultural weddings.
Alcohol & Drinks
All alcohol and drinks service is bar service only. Table service including table glassware can be arranged at an additional charge. Cherbon Waters is a BYO facility. Spirits may only be consumed within the marquee and served from behind the bar. Supply of spirits incurs a corkage fee of $440, excluding premixed spirits. Drinks delivered on the morning of the wedding must arrive cold. Opened drinks must be consumed or discarded prior to leaving the premises in accordance with liquor licensing requirements. Responsible Service of Alcohol standards apply at all times.
Music
Music must not exceed 80 decibels and bass levels must be kept to a minimum. DJs, bands, musicians and entertainers must be advised of this requirement. Cherbon Waters reserves the right to request volume reductions and, if necessary, cease music immediately in accordance with local noise restrictions.
Smoking & Vaping
Smoking and vaping are permitted only within designated areas and are prohibited in all buildings, gazebos and marquees.
